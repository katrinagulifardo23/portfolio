## Work Samples

### 1. Case Digest: *LNL Archipelago Minerals, Inc. v. AGHAM Party List*

**Area:** Philippine environmental law (Writ of Kalikasan)

A structured case digest of a Supreme Court decision on whether a mining company's port construction justified a Writ of Kalikasan.

**Includes:**

- Issue statements on tree cutting under Section 68 of PD 705 (Revised Forestry Code), the required magnitude of environmental damage, and whether a mountain was actually flattened
- Governing rules and laws: Rule 7 of A.M. No. 09-6-8-SC (Rules of Procedure for Environmental Cases), PD 705 as amended by EO 277, and Sections 57 and 69 of RA 7942 (Philippine Mining Act of 1995)
- Analysis of facts (who, what, where, how), a process flowchart, and a side-by-side table of each party's arguments
- A conclusion explaining why the petition failed: the petitioner's evidence was a lay witness and one photograph, while government and independent technical reports showed the landform was a small hill, not a mountain

**Skills shown:** Legal research, issue spotting, statutory analysis, case synthesis, and presenting complex rulings clearly.

### 2. Statement of Claim with Exhibits: Third-Party Seller v. Amazon (AAA Arbitration)

**Area:** eCommerce compliance and commercial arbitration

A Statement of Claim with three exhibits, prepared in support of supervising counsel, for an American Arbitration Association proceeding. The claimant is a third-party Amazon seller seeking release of funds held by Amazon for years without explanation.

**Includes:**

- Preliminary statement, parties, jurisdiction and venue under the Amazon Services Business Solutions Agreement (BSA), and a factual background
- Count I, breach of contract, including the argument that the BSA's liquidated damages clause is an unenforceable penalty and a contract of adhesion
- Count II, breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing, based on the lack of a competent investigation and the lack of notice or communication
- Prayer for relief covering restitution, interest, and attorneys' fees and costs
- Exhibits: a news article, the BSA, and the pre-filing Notice of Arbitration

**Skills shown:** Pleading drafting, contract analysis, exhibit organization, and familiarity with Amazon seller account and fund-withholding disputes.
